HiveMind Community Documentation

Welcome to the HiveMind community docs.

HiveMind is a community-developed extension of OpenVoiceOS, the open-source voice operating system.

With HiveMind, you can extend one OpenVoiceOS instance to many devices, including devices that cannot run OpenVoiceOS on their own.

HiveMind's developers have connected to OpenVoiceOS from a PinePhone, a 2009 MacBook, and a Raspberry Pi 0, among other devices. OpenVoiceOS itself usually runs on a desktop computer or a home server, but any Mycroft-branded device, or any OpenVoiceOS install, can act as the central unit.

Join the HiveMind Matrix chat for news, support, and general discussion.
